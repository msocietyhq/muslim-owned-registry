A listing on [muslimowned.sg](/) is a public page you write about your Singapore business so visitors can find you. Families use those pages when they are planning a kenduri, a birthday, or everyday errands. Owners use them so people who already want to support Muslim-owned businesses can reach the right mailbox.

Listing is free. You sign in with email, tell us the registered company name, the brand people know, and a public contact address. An admin reviews a UEN document or a LinkedIn profile, and then the page can be published.

## What visitors get from a page

A public listing puts the brand, the registered name, and a contact email in one place. It also shows when you last confirmed that the business is still operating, which helps someone who is booking for a date months from now.

Stories on [/blog](/blog) point you to birthday catering, a hall, or a photographer. When an article names a business, it links to the public page at `/biz/…`.

## How to get listed

1. [Sign in](/login) with a one-time code.
2. [List your business](/app/businesses/new) and accept the terms.
3. Wait for an admin to review the evidence you chose.
4. Click the link in our email to activate the listing, then confirm again every four months when we write.

If you want to browse what is already listed, start at [the directory](/browse). If you have a sentence about what you need, try [Search](/plan).
